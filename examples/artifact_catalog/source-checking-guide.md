# Checking an AI-generated learning resource

SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION — DRAFT, NOT PROFESSIONALLY REVIEWED

Start by stating what you need to learn and what would count as useful evidence.
Read the cited passage rather than relying on the citation label. Record an
important uncertainty or omission before accepting an AI-generated explanation.
Choose whether to revise the draft, obtain better evidence, or withhold judgment.

A matching content hash checks bytes, not accuracy. A recorded content review
does not authorize execution, publishing, clinical use, or institutional access.

Reflection: What did you independently notice that the AI did not establish?

This example contains no patient cases or employer documents. It has not been
evaluated for nurse learning, accessibility, or clinical use. Nothing is saved
to a private portfolio by this demonstration.
