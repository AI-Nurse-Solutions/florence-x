# SS-07A — Formative nurse walkthrough kit

Version 0.1.0. Proposed evaluation protocol; not a validated assessment instrument, competence examination, clinical study or deployment authorization. No participants or outcomes are recorded in this build. This protocol does not grant approval to conduct an institutional study.

## Purpose and boundaries

Determine whether the existing public/synthetic learning journey helps a nurse inspect evidence, deliberate, prepare a useful review note, and accurately understand system limits without excessive review burden. Primary pillar Judgment; Knowledge supplies source/uncertainty recognition, Capability supplies pause and recovery, Contribution supplies an attributable learning note and reviewed improvement proposals. The risk is ceremonial compliance: completed fields are not proof of understanding.

Target three to five voluntary formative walkthroughs when participants are available. This is a planning target, not a claim of statistical adequacy. Use a range of self-described AI familiarity and device preferences without ranking participants or collecting employment, patient or personnel records. Offer keyboard, zoom, readable text and permitted assistive technology. Do not require unaided operation as technological purity. Accessibility findings require real user input; a narrow screenshot is insufficient.

Before involving anyone, the product owner confirms permission, local arrangements, accessible participation and the purpose of feedback. No recruitment, invitation, recording or data transmission is initiated by this kit. Participation is voluntary, may stop at any point, and is not an employment or credential assessment. Avoid supervisor pressure. Use protected/compensated time where appropriate rather than expecting unpaid institutional work.

Use only this bundled nonclinical exercise. No patient stories, employer files, SOUL profiles, credentials or private portfolios. Do not copy the learner's initial interpretation or reflection into an evaluation dataset by default. Observe task behavior and capture only agreed, non-sensitive usability notes. Keep any completed observation worksheet outside source control and outside the shared Seed Garden; no automatic upload, telemetry, training or publication. Agree on custodian, storage, deletion date and access before collecting actual data. None is configured by this release.

## Facilitator opening

“We are evaluating this prototype, not you. It contains prepared proposals that can be wrong. No live AI is running, and no portfolio can be saved. Use invented or public information only. Your entries disappear on reload or clear. You may disagree, pause, stop, or skip a question. I will distinguish what you did independently from what needed a prompt. We will agree on any notes before retaining them.”

Ask the participant to describe their learning purpose. The prototype uses a fixed mission; record whether that mission fits, not a claim that personalization was performed. Confirm the observed device, build ID, viewport and input method without collecting device identifiers. Use an anonymous session label. Do not imply that a worksheet gives researcher/clinical credentials or that agreeing to usability observation authorizes wider data processing.

## Five tasks, one journey

| Task | Ask the participant | Observe before giving help | Pillar relation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Orient | “What is this mission for? What is running, where does your work live, and what is unavailable?” | Distinguishes actual browser/no model/temporary memory from local-hybrid-hosted target previews. | Knowledge constrains Capability. |
| 2. Inspect | “Open a source and explain one thing it supports and one thing it does not establish.” | Locates the passage and a substantive limitation; does not equate a working citation with truth. | Knowledge informs Judgment. |
| 3. Deliberate | “Record your interpretation or uncertainty, compare the prepared proposal, and make your own choice.” | Reveal occurs after interpretation; alternative, consequence and choice have meaning. Any outcome can be justified; agreement and rejection are not success metrics. | Judgment directs Capability. |
| 4. Prepare | “Assemble your review note. Is it useful for your personal learning? What needs correction?” | Existing choice transfers without retyping; rejected/withheld content is not endorsed; source context and disclosure remain visible; reflection excluded. | Contribution depends on all three. |
| 5. Recover and explain | “Change the evidence diagnostic; what happens to the note? Pause and resume. Explain what a reload would lose before performing it on test-only entries.” | Notices stale comparison and historical note; cannot assemble while paused/stale; restart or clear is deliberate. Explains no save, no publication, no live model, and recoverable page versus irrecoverable temporary entries. | Capability tests Judgment and returns improvement evidence. |

After the core tasks, optionally use the other nonclinical case as a transfer probe. Record that it is a second exposure, not an independent baseline. Do not reveal an “ideal answer” before recording the participant's interpretation. A withheld choice is legitimate. Ask what new evidence would change it, rather than training reflexive rejection of AI. The kit does not establish delayed retention; a separately agreed later probe would be needed.

## Observations and measures — define before seeing results

Record task result as independent, prompted, unable, declined, or not observed; record prompt count and relevant issue severity. Never map these labels to nurse competence. Record the participant's explanation separately from the facilitator's inference. Preserve disagreement in issue review.

For each task, optionally record elapsed time, inspection/review time, correction time and recovery time as separate observed values. Do not double-count overlapping intervals. Missing times stay unknown. A comparative net-time estimate needs a genuinely observed comparable non-AI baseline; without it, report workflow time only, not time saved. Optional effort and clarity ratings (0–10) are project-defined questions, not SUS or NASA-TLX scores.

Artifact usefulness means “the learner would use this unreviewed note for their own stated learning purpose, retaining its limitations,” not professional approval or publication readiness. Capture material omissions: missing applicability, source limitation, uncertainty, or unsupported generalization. Facilitator adjudication is an interpretation requiring review, not automatically generated truth.

Report actual denominators per task. A task not observed is not a pass. Declined/withdrawn sessions are not silently dropped from recruitment/completion accounting. Zero observed participants means results unavailable, not 0% success. Avoid subgroup percentages or effectiveness inference from tiny formative samples. No employee ranking, competence badge, marketing claim or public participant data.

## Proposed go / revise / hold rules

These are provisional product-review rules for this prototype, not a validated safety threshold or permission engine.

**Hold** human testing if source integrity fails, an unexpected external transmission occurs, private material appears, a control misrepresents saving/approval, or the participant cannot stop/clear as disclosed. Preserve a minimal non-sensitive issue record, contain the test, and escalate for authorized review. Never reproduce a data leak using real sensitive material.

**Revise** the affected interaction if any observed participant persistently believes a model ran, a note was saved, a learning choice granted execution authority, or a historical note remains current. Record understanding before and after correction; do not convert a corrected explanation into independent success. Revise other repeated comprehension/accessibility burdens. Critical findings cannot be averaged away by favorable ratings.

**Consider a further bounded test**, not production release, only after the product owner and an independent reviewer inspect actual walkthrough evidence, no unresolved critical issue remains, and source/choice/save distinctions are understandable with acceptable burden for the intended users. Sample size alone, passing software, or friendly feedback cannot clear G-01/G-02/G-03. Required real-host, provider, authorization and persistence evidence is separate.
