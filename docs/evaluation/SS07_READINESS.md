## What to do next

The next product decision is whether nurses can use this one learning loop, understand its limitations and find the review note useful enough to return. Conduct the kit's permitted formative walkthroughs only after its human review and arrangements are established. Without participants, keep results unknown and prioritize correcting observed interface defects rather than manufacturing scores.

In parallel, the authorized maintainer can resolve issue #16 through the permitted process. This document does not authorize an agent to retry held writes. Native-host, live-model and operational-saving work remain separately gated. A later sprint can implement the smallest allowed real loop once identity, endpoints, budget and review requirements exist; more offline contracts alone will not establish operational proof.
