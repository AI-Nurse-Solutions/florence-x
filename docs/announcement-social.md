## LinkedIn (story-first)

> **Florence Nightingale didn't just carry a lamp. She carried *evidence.***
>
> She counted, she charted, she *proved* that disciplined observation made care safer — and she made the nurse its steward. We named our project after her on purpose.
>
> Healthcare AI has stopped *answering* and started *acting* — drafting notes, placing orders, messaging patients, handing off to other agents. The question is no longer "is the model good?" It's **"who let it do that, and can you prove it?"**
>
> **Florence-X** is an open-source, governance-first control plane that answers exactly that:
> 🔒 Every consequential AI action must pass a policy gate (EDENA) before it runs — and it **fails closed.**
> 🏠 PHI never leaves the local boundary.
> 👩‍⚕️ A clinician reviews with blast radius, reversibility, and source evidence in front of them — never a bare "approve" button.
> 🧾 Every run produces an **EvidenceBundle** — the audit trail, by default.
>
> The lamp is the evidence. The discipline is the gate. The steward is still the nurse.
>
> And the **"X" is you** — the multiplier. Take the light and make it your own story: your workflows, your policies, your institution.
>
> Apache-2.0 — and you don't have to trust it. `make eval` verifies the safety claims on your machine in ~30 minutes, and we ship an adversarial suite that *tries to break them* (and publishes the bugs it finds).
>
> 📣 Announcement: https://ai-nurse-solutions.github.io/florence-x/press-release/
> ⭐ Code: https://github.com/AI-Nurse-Solutions/florence-x
>
> If you build clinical AI — or steward it at the bedside — I'd love your eyes on it.
>
> \#HealthcareAI #ClinicalAI #NursingInformatics #AIGovernance #AgenticAI #PatientSafety #HealthIT #ResponsibleAI #FHIR #DigitalHealth #OpenSource #CMIO #CNIO
